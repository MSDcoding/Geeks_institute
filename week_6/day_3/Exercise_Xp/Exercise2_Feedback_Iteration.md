**Objective: Implement a structured feedback process to improve the prototype.**



1. **Feedback Framework :**



&nbsp;   We use the Observation → Impact → Suggestion method to give clear and respectful feedback.



Observation: Describe what you see (facts only, no opinion)



Impact: Explain why it matters for the game or the team



Suggestion: Propose a solution or improvement



This structure helps the team focus on improvement, not blame and also we try to make everything connected to busines so no one take somthing personal .





**2. one example of constructive feedback on a prototype feature:**



Feature: Player movement in the prototype



Observation: The player movement feels slow during combat scenes.



Impact: This makes the gameplay less dynamic and reduces player engagement.



Suggestion: Increase movement speed slightly or add a dash ability.



**3. How Feedback Is Received and Applied**



* Feedback is shared during weekly playtest meetings or in the team chat.



* The game designer reviews feedback and decides priorities.



* The developer applies the changes in the next iteration.



* Changes are tested again in the next prototype build.



**4.Preventing Personal or Negative Feedback :** 

&nbsp;

* Focus on the feature, not the person.



* Use facts instead of emotions.



* Avoid words like “you always” or “you never”.



* Encourage questions and discussion after feedback.



* This keeps feedback professional, useful, and respectful.
