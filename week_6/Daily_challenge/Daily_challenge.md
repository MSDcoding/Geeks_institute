**Team Communication \& Collaboration**

**Scenario Analysis :----------------------------------------------------**



Our team faced communication and collaboration problems during the sprint.





1-**- Communication Audit :**



* **Communication Failure 1 :**



**--> Issue:**



The designer added a new feature without informing the developers



**--> Why it is a problem:**



This caused bugs and wasted time



**--> Immediate improvement:**



All new features must be shared in the team chat before implementation to find a solution to from both sides



* **Communication Failure 2 :**



**--> Issue:**



Tasks are overlapping and not clearly assigned no one know his role and what to do .



**--> Why it is a problem:** 



This increases stress and confusion to all team members and increase mistakes in the project and time 



**--> Immediate improvement:**



Each task should have one clear owner to do it 



* **Communication Failure 3 :**



**--> Issue:**



Team members avoid giving feedback 



--> **Why it is a problem:**



Problems stay hidden and become bigger and that bad thing beacause somthing bad is going to happend in the end



**--> Immediate improvement:**



Schedule short weekly feedback sessions and give everyone time to exprise about his opinion and idea about each other .
