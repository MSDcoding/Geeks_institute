**Lead-to-Team :**

**2- All feedback follows:**

Observation → Impact → Suggestion



* **Feedback Example 1 :**



Observation: Combat feels repetitive after a few minutes.



Impact: Player engagement drops quickly



Suggestion: Add one new enemy behavior or timing variation.



* **Feedback Example 2 :**



Observation: Loading time increases after level restart.



Impact: This breaks game flow and frustrates players.



Suggestion: Optimize asset loading or cache resources.



**3- Feedback Tracking System :**

-------- **Exercise 3: Stress Management, Conflict Resolution \& Leadership ------------------------------------------------------------**

**Major Stress Factors and Solutions :** 



1\. Technical Stress : (Pair programming and early technical tests and also Reduce feature complexity )



2\. Deadline Stress : ( Clear priorities and short milestones ,also Scope control )



3\. Interpersonal Stress : (Clear ownership ,Open communication rules and Regular check ins)



4\. Personal Stress : (Flexible hours , Realistic expectations and Psychological safety )

**Conflict Type:** Task based conflict

**Leadership Approach :**
