**Objective: Handle stress and conflict to maintain collaboration.**



1. **Main Sources of Stress and Solutions :** 



&nbsp;          1. Tight Deadlines



Stress Source:

The team has limited time to finish the playable prototype.



Solutions:



Break work into small tasks with short deadlines.



Focus only on core features and remove non-essential ideas.



&nbsp;          2. Unclear Task Ownership



Stress Source:

Team members are not always sure who is responsible for each task.



Solutions:



Clearly assign tasks to one owner.



Use a shared task list to track responsibilities.



&nbsp;          3. Ineffective Feedback



Stress Source:

Feedback is avoided or unclear, which causes mistakes and rework.



Solutions:



Schedule regular feedback sessions.



Use the Observation → Impact → Suggestion method.



4\. **Conflict Scenario :**



**Scenario :**



The game designer wants to add a new feature, but the developer believes it will delay the prototype.



**Conflict Resolution Strategy :**



&nbsp;   - **Conflict Type:**



Task-based conflict .



    **- Steps to Resolve the Conflict :**



&nbsp;---> Let both team members explain their ideas calmly.



----> Focus the discussion on project goals and deadlines.



----> Evaluate the impact of the feature on time and quality.



----> Decide together whether to include, simplify, or postpone the feature.



&nbsp;   - **Leadership Behavior to Support the Team :**



* Stay neutral and listen to all opinions.



* Encourage respectful and open communication.



* Help the team find a compromise.



* Keep the focus on collaboration, not winning arguments.
