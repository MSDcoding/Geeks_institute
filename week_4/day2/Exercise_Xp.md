**Section 1: History and Chronology**

 

           **Q1: The Crash and The Revival (1983)**

The 1983 video game crash happened because there were too many low quality games and too many consoles. People lost trust and sales dropped. Nintendo revived the industry with the NES, and super Mario became the famous mascot
