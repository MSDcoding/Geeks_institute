**Production Document and Short Version :**



**Step 1 — Project Overview**



* Project Name: Inner Light
* Genre: 2D Platformer
* Platform: PC
* Team Size: Solo developer
* Duration: 3 weeeks

------->

* Inner Light is a simple 2D platformer about a depressed character trying to become happy again.
* The game has 4 short levels. Each level represents an emotional obstacle.
* The goal is to move forward avoid hazards and collect light fragments that represent hope . 



**Step 2 — Development Report**



* The game was made using Unity 2D .
* I started by creating basic movement and jumping.
* Placeholder graphics were used to focus on gameplay.
* Levels were built one by one, with simple platforms and obstacles.
* Some features were removed to keep the project small and clear.



**Step 3 — Problems Encountered**



* Movement felt too slow at the start
* Levels were too hard early
* Collision bugs with platforms
* Limited time as a solo developer



**Step 4 — Solutions \& Adjustments**



* Adjusted gravity and jump force
* Simplified early levels
* Fixed colliders and physics settings
* Set small daily goals to manage time



**Step 5 — Post-Mortem**



1. ***What went right:***



* Movement felt smooth
* Story and gameplay matched well



***2. What went wrong:***



* Not enough testing
* Too much time on early polish



**3. Why:**



* Lack of planning and feedback.



**Step 6 — Key Learnings**



* Test gameplay early
* Keep scope small
* Simple mechanics work best
* Planning saves time
