**Designing an Internal Communication \& Feedback System**



**PART 1 – Diagnosis :**



* The main communication problems in the company come from unclear processes and lack of structure.
* First decisions are not clearly documented, so team members are confused about priorities this causes wasted time and frustration.
* Second feedback is often given too late, which makes it less useful and sometimes emotional and this creates tension between team members.
* Third, wrong communication channels are used (important topics in chat instead of meetings)and leading to misunderstandings.
* Fourth developers feel micromanaged because managers check too often without clear goalsand this reduces motivation.
* Finally designers feel ignored because their feedback is not discussed or followed up, which lowers trust in management.
* These issues mainly affect developers and designers because they work closely under pressure and depend on clear decisions.



**PART 2 – Communication Architecture**



**-------> A. Communication Channels**



1. **Daily Stand-up :**

* Purpose: Share progress and blockers
* Users: All team members
* Frequency: Daily (15 minutes)



**2. Weekly Team Meeting :**

* Purpose: Planning and alignment
* Users: Team + manager
* Frequency: Weekly



**3. Slack / Discord :**

* Purpose: Quick questions and updates
* Users: Everyone
* Frequency: Daily (async)



**4. Notion / Documentation Tool :**

* Purpose: Decisions, guidelines, tasks
* Users: Everyone
* Frequency: Updated weekly



**5. One-on-One Meetings :**

* Purpose: Personal feedback and support
* Users: Manager + individual
* Frequency: Bi-weekly



**-------> B. Rules andd Protocols**



1. Important decisions must be written in Notion
2. Chat is for quick questions and not decisions
3. Feedback is never given publicly
4. Async communication is preferred for non-urgent topics
5. Response time: max 24h on workdays
6. Meetings must have an agenda
7. Feedback must be respectful and specific
8. Conflicts are handled privately and not in meetings



**PART 3 – Feedback System**



**------> A. Types of Feedback**



* Peer-to-peer: Short, regular feedback between teammates
* Manager-to-team: Clear performance and goal feedback
* Team-to-manager: Anonymous feedback every month



------> **B. Feedback Method and SBI Model**



I choose the SBI model (Situation–Behavior–Impact) because it is clear and reduces emotions.

Example:

“In yesterdays meeting (Situation), you interrupted the designer (Behavior), which made them uncomfortable (Impact) ”



**------> C. Feedback Safety**



Psychological safety is protected by private feedback and clear rules.

Conflicts are de-escalated through one on one talks.

Toxic feedback is stopped immediately by management.



**PART 4 – Crisis Scenario**



***Immediate action:***

The manager stops the meeting and moves the discussion offline.



***Reframed feedback:***

Private feedback using SBI, focusing on improvement not blame.



***System failure:***

No rule preventing public criticism.



***Rule change:***

All negative feedback must be given privately.



**PART 5 – Measurement and Improvment :** 



1. **Feedback frequency** – Measured monthly – Add sessions if low
2. **Team satisfaction** – Survey every 2 months – Manager review
3. **Decision clarity** – Check documentation weekly – Improve notes
4. **Conflict rate** – Track incidents monthly – Training if high
5. **Meeting efficiency** – Feedback after meetings – Adjust format
