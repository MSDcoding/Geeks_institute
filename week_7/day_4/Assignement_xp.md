**-------------------------------------- Iteration \& User Testing  --------------------------------------------------------------------**





**From Prototype to Improved Gameplay**



**------- > Prototype Description :**



* The prototype is a 2D platformer game.
* The player controls a character that must reach the end of the level by jumping between platforms and avoiding obstacles.



**--------> Core gameplay mechanic :**



* Jumping and movement
* Avoiding obstacles
* The goal is simple finish the level without falling



**Part 1 Playtesting and Observation :**



***--------> Playtest Setup :***



* Tested with 2 players
* I observed silently while they played
* Each test lasted around 5–7 minutes



***--------> Player Behavior Summary :***



* Players hesitated before some jumps
* One player failed multiple times at the same obstacle
* Both players enjoyed moving and jumping



***--------> Key Observations :***



* Players were confused about where to jump next
* Timing jumps was difficult 
* Players looked frustrated after repeated failures
* Movement felt fun and responsive



***--------> Main Problems Identified :***



* Platforms were not clearly visible
* Jump difficulty was too high
* No visual guidance for the player



**Part 2 Metrics \& Feedback Analysis**



&nbsp;- **Chosen Metrics** :



1. Completion rate
2. Time to finish level
3. Number of falls (failures)
4. Drop off point



  **- Collected Data :**



* Completion rate 1 out of 2 players
* Average time 6 minutes
* Average failures 5–7 falls
* Drop-off point Same difficult jump area



  **- Interpretation :**



* Low completion rate shows high difficulty
* High failure rate confirms player frustration
* Feedback matched metrics (players said jumps were hard)



  **- What Data Reveals :**



* The gameplay is fun  but the difficulty is too high for new players.



**Part 3 Iteration \& Improvement 	:**



----> ***Problem → Solution Table :***



* Jumps too hard ***→*** Increase jump height
* No guidance	***→***   Add visual cues
* High frustration  ***→***	Add checkpoint



----> ***Implemented Changes:***



* Increased jump force
* Added arrows showing jump direction
* Added a checkpoint before hard obstacle



***----> Before and  After Comparison :***



* ***Before*** Players failed often and felt lost
* ***After*** Gameplay is clearer and less frustrating



----> ***Next Iteration Plan :***



* Test again with 2 new players
* Measure completion rate again
* Adjust difficulty if needed



***----> Final Reflection :***



* ***What surprised me*** : Players struggled more than expected
* ***What changed most*** : Level difficulty and clarity
* ***If I had more time*** : I would add better tutorials and animations
