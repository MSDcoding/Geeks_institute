--> Context:



During my bootcamp, I worked on a team mini-project where we had to design and develop a small

game. My role was to implement the player mechanics and integrate them into the main scene. At

one point, there was a delay in my task because I underestimated the time needed to fix a physics

issue. This caused a small slowdown in the overall progress of the team.





--> Emotional Awareness:



When I realized the delay, I initially felt stressed and a bit frustrated with myself. I was worried that I

might disappoint my teammates. Instead of reacting emotionally, I took a step back, reviewed the

issue carefully, and focused on finding a practical solution. I reminded myself that challenges are

part of learning.





--> Communication \& Empathy:



I informed my team lead as soon as I understood the situation. I explained clearly what was causing

the delay and gave an updated realistic timeline. I also listened to my teammates’ concerns and

understood that their tasks depended on mine. We discussed how to reorganize priorities

temporarily so the project could continue smoothly.





--> Mindset \& Responsibility:



I took full responsibility for the delay. I learned that task estimation is a critical professional skill and

that communicating early is always better than staying silent. Next time, I would break tasks into

smaller parts and give a buffer when estimating deadlines.
