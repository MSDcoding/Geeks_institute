**Part 1 — Paper Prototype:**

For my paper prototype, I tested my core stealth gameplay loop before using Unity i used simple tokens for the player and cards for enemies, and squares for rooms .

**Core Loop**  :  Sneak → Hear danger → Hide → Stay safe → Move forward

**Main Verb** : The player’s main verb is hiding and sneaking

**Win / Lose Condition** : --> Win: The player reaches the exit door without being caught.

&nbsp;                      --> lose : The player gets seen by the enemy

**Biggest Issue Found** : The biggest problem during playtesting was that it was sometimes unclear where the player could hide .

**Change I Made** : I added more hiding spots and a warning moment before enemies detect the player.



**Part 2 — Blockout / Greyboxing (Level + Feel Prototyping)**

In Unity, I built a simple greybox level using only cubes and basic shapes. The goal was to quickly test movement and stealth and space layout.



**What Worked Well :**

The narrow hallways supported the feeling of tension and made sneaking more interesting.

**What Needs Redesign :**

Some areas felt too open so the player had nowhere to hide.

**Feedback That Changed My Layout :**

After testing I added more cover objects and made the path clearer so players understood where to go next.
